Architected an AI-powered photo management platform using **Django, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, S3, and Pinecone**, achieving **P95 retrieval latency below 720 ms** over a benchmark of **1,000 indexed images**.

Developed an image understanding pipeline using **Apple FastVLM** for caption generation, face embedding extraction for identity recognition, and automated album tagging.

Implemented cross-modal image retrieval by indexing caption embeddings in **Pinecone** and matching natural-language queries against visual content.
