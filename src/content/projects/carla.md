Built a CARLA data generation pipeline that produced **1M+ multimodal driving samples**, including RGB, depth, vehicle state, control signals, and waypoints for imitation learning experiments.

Established a reproducible benchmarking framework to evaluate **6+ vision architectures** including ResNet, 3D ResNet, and R(2+1)D for control regression and waypoint prediction.

Analyzed large-scale driving datasets to identify waypoint generation failures, class imbalance, and collection artifacts, guiding iterative improvements to data quality and model behavior.
